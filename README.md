# CyberOps-Lab

A hands-on cybersecurity learning journey documenting foundational knowledge, practical defensive skills, security investigations, and SOC Analyst development.

## About This Repository

Welcome to **CyberOps-Lab**. This repository is my learning and portfolio space as I build cybersecurity knowledge and practical defensive security skills, following the topics in the TechCrush Cybersecurity Bootcamp Curriculum supplied for this learning project.

The goal is to develop the knowledge, habits, and documentation skills needed for entry-level Security Operations Center (SOC) work.

All practical activities are intended for systems, virtual machines, and datasets that I own or have explicit permission to use. Where appropriate, exercises will use fictional scenarios and synthetic training data.

## Portfolio Project: SOC Analyst Professional Simulation

The planned capstone is a **SOC Analyst Professional Simulation** based on a fictional organization. It will bring together the defensive concepts and workflows developed during the curriculum.

Potential deliverables, to be confirmed during the capstone planning stage, include:
- A fictional organization and scenario overview
- Alert-triage notes
- An investigation report based on supplied training evidence
- An incident response record
- A threat-intelligence summary where relevant
- Defensive recommendations and a lessons-learned report
- Final project presentation materials

The capstone will use safe, authorized, and preferably synthetic evidence. The exact scope will be defined during Week 9 planning rather than assumed in advance.

## How I Document My Progress

For each completed learning milestone, I aim to include:
- Clear notes explaining the concepts in my own words
- Definitions of important technical terms
- Practical exercises and their objectives
- The steps taken and the results observed
- Review questions, answers, and reflections
- Reports explaining findings and recommended defensive improvements

I will distinguish between simulated exercises, observations, and verified results. I will not claim to have performed an activity unless I have actually completed it.

## Learning and Completion Principles

- Lessons will be studied in sequence, one step at a time.
- Concepts will be explained before practical instructions are given.
- Practical results will be reviewed before moving to the next activity.
- Lesson notes will be prepared after the lesson has been taught and completed.
- The repository will reflect actual progress, not planned or unfinished work.

## Safety and Responsible Practice

This repository is for education and defensive cybersecurity.

- Only assess systems that I own or have explicit permission to assess.
- Use approved training environments and synthetic datasets for practical exercises.
- Do not publish passwords, API keys, access tokens, private keys, personal information, or confidential organizational data.
- Do not upload sensitive logs, private screenshots, or identifying information.
- Keep demonstrations focused on understanding, prevention, detection, and response.
